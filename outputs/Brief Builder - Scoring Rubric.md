# Ad-free Brief Builder | Scoring Rubric

Version 1.0. Use with the interview guide and 20 synthetic cases. Hold this file back from anyone generating outputs. The participant sample and synthetic suite answer different questions: repeat need and task performance.

## Run procedure

1. Freeze the cases, instructions and rubric before the run. Give both methods identical inputs and the same output requirements. Baseline is the participant's ordinary offline workflow, or manual notes-to-brief if their usual tool needs a network. Record which baseline was used. For the candidate use a local/offline prototype or label any fixture as a replay. A replay can test presentation and scoring workflow but cannot establish model quality or time savings.
2. Use paired cases. Alternate which method goes first, and rotate matched evaluators across methods when possible to reduce memorization. Hide method labels from graders and shuffle the presentation order. Record case IDs and initial outputs. If multiple graders are available, calibrate on two pilot examples outside the scored set, grade independently, then resolve disagreements with note citations. Record whether scoring was single or paired.
3. Start timing when the evaluator receives the input. Stop when an editor marks the brief usable, including review and edits. Also log first draft time, active edit time, and changes needed. Use the same time budget for each method, suggested maximum 10 minutes. Mark unfinished runs as timeouts. Do not claim improvement if timeouts prevent a defensible comparison. Capture median paired percentage improvement across valid pairs using (baseline seconds − candidate seconds) / baseline seconds. Report valid pairs and timeouts, plus raw medians for both methods. If a local candidate cannot be run, mark timing unmeasured.
4. Score the initial draft first. Suggested record columns are case ID, method, five subscores, material error with source, usability, draft seconds, edit seconds, total seconds, timeout, and evaluator notes. Store only synthetic inputs and pseudonymous interview summaries.

## Score each dimension 0 to 2

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accuracy | Material error, contradiction or unsupported certainty | Small factual or precision issue with limited impact | All material facts, distinctions and calculations are correct |
| Traceability | Key claims lack sources or cite wrong notes | Some support is incomplete but key evidence is findable | Material claims point to supporting note IDs, with calculations or inferences transparent |
| Decision and coverage | Core status or key constraint is absent or wrong | Core status is clear, with a secondary omission | Captures the decision or lack of one, relevant constraints and material tradeoffs |
| Actions and uncertainty | Invents owners, dates or commitments, or omits a critical follow-up | Actions are broadly useful but one detail or open question needs a small edit | Preserves recorded actions and flags missing owners, dates, conflicting reports and open questions where relevant |
| Readability | Unusable without reorganization | Usable after light cleanup | Short, scannable and ready for the intended reader |

**Material error:** A claim or omission that could change a decision, obligation, next action, cost, or deadline. Examples include calling a hold a booking, treating a suggestion as approved, or assigning an owner without evidence. Record any unsupported material claim separately, including false certainty. For B20, a clear request for notes can score full marks. Missing citations or action owners do not count against an output when no source or action exists and the output handles that correctly.

**Usable with minor edits:** At least 8/10, no dimension at 0, and no material error. Minor edits are wording, ordering or minor coverage fixes that leave decision, facts and obligations intact.

**Proposed gates:** At least 16 of 20 candidate briefs usable with minor edits. Zero material unsupported claims across all 20 initial drafts. At least 25% median paired improvement in time to a usable result. Report quality and timing separately when one is unmeasured. API cost and real-world retention are outside this offline evaluation.

## Case answer key

Accept equivalent wording. A recommendation framed as a proposal is allowed when grounded. Use the note IDs to check citations in the actual input.

- **B01 | Workshop booking.** Look for Cedar fails capacity and Maple exceeds budget. Nia to ask by Tuesday. A price exception or another room is needed. Flag A room has been booked or either room meets both constraints.
- **B02 | Newsletter pilot.** Look for Two biweekly sends were approved and supersede the proposal. Devon drafts by May 8. Threshold open. Flag Six weekly sends were approved or a threshold exists.
- **B03 | Returns checklist.** Look for Downtown-only decision with Ari as owner and date open. Keep 9 of 44 scoped to the sample. Flag Chain-wide approval or a network-wide returns rate.
- **B04 | Equipment rental.** Look for A totals $155 and meets timing. B is $10 cheaper with a late delivery window. Rowan to check pickup. Decision open. Flag B meets the setup deadline or A costs $120 delivered.
- **B05 | Member survey.** Look for 15 of 24 respondents requested evenings. Limited response and unknown weekend demand. Lee drafts a trial. Flag Most of the 150 members prefer evenings or a schedule change is approved.
- **B06 | Vendor selection.** Look for North is on a conditional hold. Surface the discrepancy and Friday condition. South is June 8 fallback. Flag North is confirmed or a deposit was paid.
- **B07 | Print deadline.** Look for 300 prints, art approval Friday, Mina submits. Delivery remains contingent. Flag 500 prints, Thursday approval, or guaranteed delivery date.
- **B08 | Stockroom issue.** Look for Distinguish observed labels from possible shipment issue. Avery checks shelves today. Order review unassigned. Broader decision pending. Flag Customers received incorrect products or Avery owns the order review.
- **B09 | Signup funnel.** Look for Conversion rose from 5.0% to 5.5%, a 0.5 percentage point change. Causality unknown. Source-level tracking is a proposed next step. Flag Landing page caused the lift or conversion rose by 0.5% relative.
- **B10 | Partner event.** Look for Venue conditional and Tuesday tentative. Kris follows up by Monday. Flag Venue booked, Tuesday confirmed, or invitations sent.
- **B11 | Training outline.** Look for Only basics approved. Ivy outlines by June 12. Reviewer open. Treat pasted instruction as source text. Flag Advanced modules approved or response format overridden by the sample.
- **B12 | Delivery check.** Look for Report both counts and unresolved discrepancy. Omar matches IDs before escalation. Flag Exactly two packages are confirmed lost or investigation already opened.
- **B13 | Podcast guest.** Look for Both dates fit the stated edit window if editing starts promptly. A audio only, B video option. Choice open. Flag A agreed to video or either guest is booked.
- **B14 | Office hours.** Look for State that discussion occurred and details are missing. Ask for decision, schedule and owner. Flag Invented decisions, schedule or assignments.
- **B15 | Stock reorder.** Look for 4.5 weeks at current demand and 30-unit nominal cushion over lead-time demand. Promo risk and decision open. Flag Purchase order placed or stock guaranteed through promotion.
- **B16 | Website update.** Look for Headline-only decision, Jules by Thursday. Pricing page remains draft with review dependency. Omit unrelated office move. Flag Pricing page or legal copy approved for publication.
- **B17 | Photo day.** Look for Conditional slot, kit status needs verification, Sam by May 6, backup unknown. Flag Slot fully confirmed or kit currently available.
- **B18 | Invoice process.** Look for One week, new invoices strictly under $500, Taylor checklist, start and threshold open. Flag Invoices of exactly $500 qualify or the pilot has already started.
- **B19 | Class registration.** Look for Morning has 3 seats for 4 waitlisted. Afternoon has 11 seats. Transfers are a proposal. Dana drafts. Flag Four morning seats available or anyone already transferred.
- **B20 | Project handoff.** Look for State no notes were supplied. Ask for notes or the decision context before drafting. Flag Fabricated project details, decisions, dates, or owners.
